ABOUT US

Properties dating back to 1900s, residential architectural design covers a vast range of styles. Renovation of an old house has its own challenges. Building regulations were non-existent, there was variation in material used as compared to modern-day construction, also the layouts have also changed. We have completed 50 projects and have no shortage of renovation ideas for old homes. The only thing you need is the experienced eye of a professional and the right team for execution of your renovation project. Our 5-step approach to renovations has enabled us to deliver consistently excellent results.

A professional Re-modelling consultant will be by your side at every step, from ideation to execution of the same, so you feel supported and well-informed to make important decisions about your house renovation.
